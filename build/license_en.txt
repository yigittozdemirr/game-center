GameCenter - license and privacy

PRIVACY
GameCenter runs on your PC. It has no account, no telemetry and no server of its own.

To show game info and artwork, it sends the names of your installed games (and Steam app IDs) to:
  - the Steam store (store.steampowered.com, api.steampowered.com, steamstatic.com),
  - Wikipedia / Wikidata (for games that aren't on Steam),
  - the libretro thumbnail archive (thumbnails.libretro.com, emulator covers only).
No other information about you or your PC is sent.

You can turn this off at any time: Settings > General > "Download game info and artwork from the internet". When it's off, GameCenter sends nothing on its own; games are shown with their local icons.

Microsoft / Xbox Live is contacted only if you connect an Xbox account yourself. Its sign-in token is stored encrypted on your PC and deleted when you disconnect.

WHAT THE INSTALLER CHANGES
  - Installs GameCenter for your Windows user (default: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\GameCenter).
  - Creates desktop and Start menu shortcuts.
  - Adds GameCenter to Settings > Apps, where you can uninstall it at any time.
  - "Start with Windows" is off unless you turn it on in Settings.
Your library and settings are kept in %APPDATA%\gamecenter, screenshots in Pictures\GameCenter. Uninstalling keeps them.
